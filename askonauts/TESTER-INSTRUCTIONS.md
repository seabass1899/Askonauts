# ASKONAUTS Shadow Box — Parent Playtest Instructions

Thank you for helping us test ASKONAUTS.

1. Open the link we send you.
2. Choose your child's age range and tap **START PLAYTEST**.
3. Hand the device to your child.
4. Say only: **“See what you can figure out.”**
5. Please do not explain what Quip, the light, or the switch does unless your child becomes completely stuck.
6. When your child naturally wants to stop, tap **Parent** in the bottom-right corner of the screen (it works in every world).
7. Tap **FINISH TEST** and answer four quick questions.

That's all. Game interaction information saves automatically. You do not need to download or send us any files.
